Key Considerations for Borrowers

When evaluating finance options, borrowers are encouraged to look beyond just the headline interest rate. ASIC Moneysmart advises a thorough comparison of loan options, encompassing all associated costs and repayment structures. This includes understanding the implications of features like offset accounts or the choice between fixed and variable interest rates. For those considering refinancing an existing loan, it's important to factor in any switching costs to determine if a change will genuinely result in savings.

For property investors, the conversation with a broker should remain focused on the specifics of investment borrowing. This means providing clear documentation and understanding what assumptions still require lender confirmation. For business, commercial, and equipment finance, the broker should be able to articulate which lenders are most likely to consider the application favourably, based on factors such as business records, the purpose of the asset being financed, or the required security.

Questions to Refine Your Loan Shortlist

To ensure a productive discussion with a finance broker, preparing specific questions can significantly narrow down the options and lead to a more suitable loan. For home and investment loans, ask about the specific features that are most relevant to your situation, such as offset accounts or redraw facilities. Clarify which fees are most critical to compare and understand any potential switching costs if you are refinancing.

When discussing investment property loans, focus on the evidence required to present your case to lenders and what aspects of the scenario are subject to lender assessment. For business, commercial, and equipment finance, inquire about the lender's typical focus - whether it's on financial statements, the asset's purpose, or property security. It is also beneficial to ask which type of borrowing the broker arranges most frequently, which lenders are best suited for your specific scenario, and what fees, features, or repayment issues should be prioritised in your comparison.
